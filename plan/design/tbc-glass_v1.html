Computed from the hex codes for the light look. Glass panels let the background show through; text sits on near-white glass, so these ratios hold only when the panel is at least 58% white, which is the setting here. I did not measure text placed directly on a scene.

  • Long text and forms sit on near-solid white glass; ribbons, discs and spheres stay behind panels.
  • Mint and lime are fills with black text. Never mint or lime text on the light page.
  • Main action is the emerald raised button. Mint marks the moment of connection. Black marks stop and errors. No red anywhere.
