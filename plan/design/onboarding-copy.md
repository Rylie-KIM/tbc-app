## Before the questions (three short screens, one idea each)

**Screen 1: What TBC is for**
- Title: *Sponsors and hackathons, in one place*
- Text: *TBC helps people who run hackathons find sponsors, and helps sponsors find events worth backing. No more searching through emails and PDFs.*
- Button: *Next*

**Screen 2: Two kinds of user**
- Title: *Two sides, and you can try both*
- Card **Organiser**: *You run a hackathon or student event and need support: money, credits, prizes, mentors or a venue.*
- Card **Sponsor**: *You decide whether your company backs events: to hire, to get product feedback, or to be seen.*
- Line under the cards: *You picked [Organiser/Sponsor]. You can switch any time with the button in the top right corner.*
- Button: *Next*

**Screen 3: Why we are asking**
- Title: *A few questions about sponsoring*
- Text: *We are building TBC around what sponsors actually need, so we would like to learn how you decide which events to back. It takes about a minute. There are no right answers.*
- Small print: *Your answers are saved to help us design TBC. Your email is optional. [What we store] [Delete my answers]*
- Buttons: *Start* and *Skip and explore*

**If the person chose Organiser (screen 3 changes)**
- Title: *We are starting with sponsors*
- Text: *This first round is about what sponsors need. You can answer two or three quick questions about how you find sponsors today, or go straight to exploring.*
- Buttons: *Answer a few questions* and *Skip and explore*

Note on bias: these screens explain the product before the questions. Keep screen 1 to one general sentence, and compare these answers with the Google Form answers by source (F21.11).

## After the questions: guided start

**Welcome card (start page)**
- Title: *You are ready to explore*
- Text: *Everything here is example data, so try anything. Want a quick tour?*
- Buttons: *Show me around* and *I'll explore myself*

**Tour steps, Sponsor side**
1. Role switch (top right): *Switch sides here to see TBC as an organiser too.*
2. Filters: *Filter events by goal, place, size and who organises them.*
3. An example event: *Open an event to see its packages and its prospectus.*
4. Prospectus viewer: *Read it here, or download the PDF.*
5. Propose button: *Found a fit? Send a proposal for a package.*
6. Proposals: *Replies and counter-offers appear here.*

**Tour steps, Organiser side**
1. Role switch (top right): *Switch sides here to see TBC as a sponsor too.*
2. Add event: *Start with your prospectus, or try one of our samples.*
3. Sample download: *Download a sample and upload it back to see how reading works.*
4. Review screen: *Check what we read from the file and fix anything that is wrong.*
5. Packages: *Set your tiers, prices and what each includes.*
6. Proposals: *Sponsors' proposals and your replies appear here.*

**Closing checklist card ("Try these three things")**
- Sponsor: *Open an example event* / *Send a proposal* / *Switch to the organiser side*
- Organiser: *Try a sample prospectus* / *Review what was read* / *Switch to the sponsor side*
- Footer: *Skip, hide this card, or replay the tour any time from the menu (Show me around).*
